### 👨‍💻 About Me
I'm a Computer Science graduate (BSc, University of Western Macedonia, 2026). My background covers networking, Linux, IT infrastructure and hardware, and for my thesis I looked at how local large language models can help analyze software for suspicious behavior.

A few areas I enjoy working in:

- **Networking:** Troubleshooting, network services and how traffic actually moves.
- **Systems:** Daily Linux user (Kali, Arch, Ubuntu) and comfortable around servers and SSH.
- **Hardware:** Building and configuring my own setups, including a custom Raspberry Pi Cyberdeck.
- **AI & LLMs:** Applying machine learning and local language models to practical problems.
