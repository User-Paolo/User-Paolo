### 👨‍💻 About Me
I'm a Computer Science graduate (BSc, University of Western Macedonia). My background covers networking, Linux, IT infrastructure and hardware, and for my thesis I looked at how local large language models can help analyze software for suspicious behavior.

A few areas I enjoy working in:

- 🌐 **Networking:** Troubleshooting, network services and how traffic actually moves.
- 🐧 **Systems:** Daily Linux user (Kali, Arch, Ubuntu) and comfortable around servers and SSH.
- 🔧 **Hardware:** Building and configuring my own setups, including a custom Raspberry Pi Cyberdeck.
- 🤖 **AI & LLMs:** Applying machine learning and local language models to real problems, not just demos.

### 📂 What You'll Find Here
- 🎓 **Bachelor Thesis:** Research code, datasets and results from my thesis on using local large language models to analyze Windows executables.
- 🏫 **University Projects:** Coursework from my degree, uploaded with a few additions such as tests, CI and clearer documentation.
- 🛠️ **Personal Projects:** Tools and experiments I build in my own time, including hackathon entries.
- 🧪 **Labs (coming soon):** Hands on practice with networking, Linux and system administration.
